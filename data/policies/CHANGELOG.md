# Synthetic Healthcare Policy Dataset Changelog

All policies in this dataset are fictional and were created for
software development, demonstration and evaluation.

The dataset does not contain real payer policies, medical guidance or
protected health information.

## Version 1.0.0 — 2026-10-04

### Added

- Lumbar Spine MRI
- Cervical Spine MRI
- Knee MRI
- Chest CT
- Outpatient Physical Therapy
- Positive Airway Pressure Equipment
- Home Health Services
- Elective Inpatient Admission
- Specialty Biologic Medication
- Colonoscopy Services

### Dataset guarantees

- Every policy has a stable policy ID.
- Every policy contains source and version metadata.
- Every policy requires human review.
- No policy supports autonomous authorization.
- All data and organizations are synthetic.
